abstract: >
  
  I firmly believe 52 Bars was the beginning of Karan Aujla's second resurgence. If we take a look at his initial resurgence, it started with the song Don't Worry with Gurlez Akhtar and Deep Jandu, wherein he began to be recognized within the Punjabi industry as a top talent. However, it wasn't really until this song that he really started to find a voice of his own. Now, he has released songs similar to this before where he is doing a freestyle of sorts, such as Facts in 2019, and It's Okay God in April 2020 (Very much underrated!). This is where his lyrical genius can shine, and there are some intentional lyrical choices that I wanted to point out and appreciate, as well as an English translation.

  The first interesting line that I want to point out here is the line ""

  Yet another interesting line to translate is here: "Hunn mitran nu shaunk hai ni race’an laun da ni", translating to "My friends are big enjoyers of running (read: partaking in) races". This is especially interesting when we look at the partnership he started later that year with Stake. Now, obviously, I am of the camp that this sponsorship is not a good thing especially given how many young people look up to him, but I digress. The point is that even though Stake owns a F1 team, Karan is talking about horse racing, of which he is actually the jockey. We can see this in the next line, but let's take another paragraph to look carefully at it.
  
  Karan says "Ni meri khich la lagam mainu aankhe nukri", translating roughly to "Even if you pull my reins, my eyes won't budge".
  
  Here's my english translation that tries to keep it as faithful to the original Punjabi as much as possible. I'll try to break down some of the more lyrically complex lines as well into a more palatable version. Enjoy!
